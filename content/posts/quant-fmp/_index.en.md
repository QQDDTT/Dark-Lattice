The Quant-FMP column is dedicated to the engineering and systematic research of full-market US equities quantitative forecasting and automated execution systems.

Here, we thoroughly explore:
- **Full US Market Data Engineering**: Zero survivor-bias pipelines covering active and delisted tickers, adaptive token-bucket rate limiting, DuckDB + Apache Parquet columnar data lakes, and tiered hot/cold storage.
- **Quantitative Time-Series & Feature Extraction**: 86-dimensional high-precision price/volume and cross-industry spillover features, unsupervised dual-clustering adaptive sampling, and strictly lookahead-free point-in-time alignments.
- **Multi-Target Specialized Model Matrix**: Six-segment standard model naming, self-contained reproducible bundle directories, LightGBM expert models with dynamic news calibration, and Purged K-Fold validation with early stopping.
- **Trading Safety & Dual-Track Verification**: "Safety First" five-layer defense infrastructure, forward out-of-sample delivery accounting against unobserved futures, and zero-build vanilla web architecture.
