The Evotensor Workspace Architecture column documents the foundational transformation of our multi-project engineering workspace from a collection of discrete repositories into a **self-organizing, autopoietic engineering ecosystem**.

Here, we systematically share our recent major architectural milestones:
- **Taxonomy Unification & The Storage-Compute-Edge Triad**: Eliminating the ambiguous "Ordinary" category in favor of **Four Orthogonal Archetypes (Console / Foundation / Platform / Showcase)**; enforcing the strict separation of Google Drive data storage, ephemeral GCP Spot compute (`Destroy-on-Exit` with zero long-running services), and isolated `*-UI` GitHub Pages static hosting, alongside the mandatory **SPEC-17 Global UI Terminal Adaptation Standard**.
- **Eight Foundation Pillars & Global Skill Matrix**: Deconstructing how `Evopoiesis`, `Aether-Ops`, `Chaos-Matrix`, `Optic-Resonance`, `Momus-Audit`, `Harness-Forge`, `Aegis-Sec`, and `Chroma-Muse` empower every project via single-canonical-source host-level skill injection.
- **Generational Leap in Console & Platforms**: Reviewing the retirement of legacy `Aura` and traditional `TeachingSolution` repositories in favor of **Evo-Studio** (an AI-center-stage autonomous IDE workshop with dual-tier cognitive routing) and **Syntax-Lab** (a pure-frontend time-travel stepping laboratory for language internals, algorithms, and distributed architectures), alongside updates across all 12 core platforms including `Nex-Micro`, `Nomos-Core`, `Quant-FMP`, and `Quant-FX` v4.0.
