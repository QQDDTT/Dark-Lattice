The Gestalt Resonator column focuses on the "design middleware" technology for Agent collaboration. Here, we combine design composition, Gestalt psychology principles, and software engineering models to explore how to achieve seamless translation from "requirements definition" to "high-fidelity code".

We explore visual gravity control, proximity grouping (Proximity Groups), and "Common Fate" physical motion effects to provide a brand new theoretical foundation for intelligent agent collaboration and modern frontend engineering.
