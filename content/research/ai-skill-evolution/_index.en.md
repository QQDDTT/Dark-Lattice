This research topic uses **"human mastery and cognitive depth of Artificial Intelligence"** as its primary variable to establish a 7-tier AI Capability Ladder (L1–L7). By quantifying demographic trajectories, purchasing power archetypes, and budgetary leverage, this research projects market demand migration and technical paradigm evolution from 2024 to 2035.
