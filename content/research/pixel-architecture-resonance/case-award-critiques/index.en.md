## 🎯 4. Cross-Domain Underlying Resonance Nurtured in Review Videos

After summarizing the above 8 most representative review videos, it can be found that they all deeply elaborate on the core interdisciplinary viewpoints:

1.  **Gestalt is the Foundation of Advanced Visual Design**:
    Whether in the grid alignment of Awwwards winning websites (Jenny Does Designs) or guiding the audience's line of sight through smooth camera continuity in the *Flow* animation (Gints Zilbalodis), they are all conforming to the \"continuity\" and \"closure\" principles in visual psychology, ensuring that complex dynamic scenes still maintain a harmonious sense of the whole.
2.  **Strong Support of Technical Engineering Workflows (SDLC/Workflow) for Artistic Creativity**:
    When decoding the practical cases of Build in Amsterdam, Julian Mollema emphasizes that high-dimensional brand aesthetics must be translated into modular front-end components, styling systems, and precise workflow management. This is precisely the use of the object-oriented method (OOD) of \"combination-abstraction-construction\" in IT engineering planning to perform strict lifecycle logical control over complex visual forms.
