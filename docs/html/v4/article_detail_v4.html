Migrating to the New V4 Engine

Published on October 12, 2026

In this release, we are completely overhauling our design system to embrace the Gestalt principles. By removing physical borders and relying entirely on HSL grayscale steps, we achieve a cleaner and more focused user interface.

The Token System

Our color palette is now strictly defined in a 5-layer grayscale system. Each layer is approximately 3% lighter in HSL lightness than the one below it. This provides enough contrast to distinguish depth without introducing visual noise.

--bg-base: hsl(220, 13%, 8%);
--bg-surface: hsl(220, 12%, 11%);
--bg-elevated: hsl(220, 11%, 15%);

Performance First

Animations are restricted to transform and opacity. No more layout trashing. Everything runs at 60fps, even on low-end devices. Our cubic-bezier curves provide a natural, snappy feel that respects physics.
